# Private Markets

## Architecture decision

**Status** Accepted for development. Live distribution and document processing require review.

The iOS feature uses the existing navigation, portfolio, currency service, and Supabase session. An Edge Function serves the public catalogue. It requires a valid session for company updates, research, investor profiles and provider handoff. Supabase stores published facts and scoped account records. The iPhone stores manual holdings, valuations, cash flows and watchlists in account-specific, data-protected files excluded from backups. Signed-out device records stay in their own scope. The listed portfolio keeps its original daily performance history and gains a unified allocation that uses dated private carrying values.

This design reuses the existing AI gateway, model credentials, quota and encryption envelope. The server fetches selected evidence by authenticated ownership, passes excerpts only after explicit permission and a deployment flag, resolves numbered source citations, and stores reports with the same envelope encryption. Committee runs continue on the Edge runtime while the app polls an account-scoped status endpoint. Source documents and analyst outputs are untrusted inputs. The language models receive no application or order tools. A specialist review or synthesis failure fails the committee run. Quotes are checked mechanically. Analysis and its interpretation still require review.

## Phase 1 · Discovery, research and tracking

- The catalogue includes closed Grong Grong Solar Farm rounds, Heidi’s Series A follow-on, B and C, and AdvanCell’s Series C and D. It also includes public offer references for Alvear Tungsten and MoPA with unverified availability. Publish additional facts only with a public issuer source or contractual publication rights. Set publication flags on the opportunity, round and company. Use the original source URL, record the verification timestamp and leave unavailable fields empty. When a listing or round passes its 24-hour verification window or its deadline, in-app handoff is unavailable.
- Funding Rounds lists verified active rounds by deadline and historical rounds by announcement date. Startup Explorer shows the published history for each company, with original sources, raised amounts and reported valuations where disclosed. Compare funding rounds opens the research workspace with up to three recent sources selected. Historical announcement dates are recorded separately from deadlines and verification timestamps.
- Signed-in users can search the public catalogue in natural language. The model interprets the question as filters. The server applies the filters to published records, including current availability, eligibility and currency checks. The interface displays the interpreted filters and any constraints that need clarification.
- A maintained provider directory links to Birchal, OnMarket, AngelList Meridian and Syndex. These links lead to each provider's public website or documentation.
- Research uses one to three published opportunities, company announcements or approved excerpts. Deal Room extracts selectable PDF text and recognizes text on scanned PDF pages on the device. It also accepts UTF-8 files and pasted text. Only the reviewed excerpt is uploaded. The limit is 4,500 characters, with scan recognition attempted on up to eight pages. Provider data-room ingestion requires provider permission. Previously generated reports can be removed independently of their sources.
- AI Investment Research restores the account’s 20 most recent running or failed attempts from the last seven days. It checks for completion while the screen is open and moves completed runs to Saved Reports. An attempt still marked running after three minutes displays a timeout. Switching accounts clears the prior account’s drafts, source selections, results and saved item titles from the workspace.
- Unified allocation aggregates tracked listed portfolios with self-reported and provider-confirmed private positions using dated marks and available FX. Copilot can show the same allocation in response to an exposure question. That request is handled on the device. A position without a carrying value or FX rate is excluded and counted in the interface. A stale mark is flagged after 90 days. Cash-flow and valuation dates govern XIRR, MOIC and TVPI. DPI uses settled fund distributions. XIRR returns unavailable when the recorded cash flows do not establish a unique, bracketed rate. Provider performance also requires the evidenced opening settlement and complete activity history.
- Manual holdings can link to a Deal Room excerpt and record the owner's review. The holding remains self-reported. Changing the recorded ownership terms clears the link and review date. Manual holdings can also link to a published catalogue company for monitoring.
- Company updates show curated and automatically imported issuer announcements and recently verified funding events for followed companies, linked manual holdings and provider-confirmed holdings. Each update includes a source, timestamp and significance. Users can ask the research agent to summarise up to three published announcements at a time. The agent receives only those published records.
- A scheduled refresh reads AdvanCell’s public newsroom and uses the issuer’s canonical links and original publication dates to publish categorized notices. It stores the app’s own description and source link. Each notice contains an original topic description, with financial terms and eligibility still sourced from the catalogue and providers. The initial archive is limited to the most recent ten newsroom entries, with a 180-day date limit. The monitoring inbox alerts on publications from the last seven days.
- Optional server monitoring collects published updates, due provider capital calls, provider-recorded distributions and provider-confirmed full exits every 15 minutes, including when the device is offline. A device shares up to 250 catalogue company IDs when its owner enables monitoring. The separate eligibility toggle matches newly verified open rounds against the user's current provider evidence and agreement. Device subscriptions and alerts expire after 90 days. Turning off the last subscribed device clears the account inbox. Automated ingestion from licensed feeds requires partner access.
- For a full exit, the owner’s inbox shows the provider’s report time, settlement date and source. The collector picks up reports from the previous seven days. A correction updates the evidence on the same alert. A reversal that restores units hides the alert and cancels push eligibility for the reversed event.
- A distribution alert shows the provider’s report time, payment date and statement source to the opted-in owner. The collector picks up provider reports from the previous seven days. A correction updates the original alert. A reversal removes the payment from the current portfolio ledger, hides the alert and cancels any unsent push. Daily AI summaries use published announcements only.
- When APNs is provisioned, the notifications toggle registers the current iPhone and sign-in session for a generic push that opens Company Updates. Lock-screen copy omits company, portfolio and obligation details. Tokens are encrypted and a push worker checks preferences, the active session and source availability again before sending. The app uses local notifications on refresh when remote registration is unavailable. Apple delivery is best-effort.
- Daily AI summaries require a separate device opt-in. Each account can receive one report per Sydney calendar day. A report cites up to three newly observed company announcements from the last seven days, based on catalogue IDs shared by devices with active consent. The model receives only the public announcements. Reports use the shared research quota, are encrypted, and appear in Company Updates and Saved Reports. Turning summaries off stops future processing for that device. Users can delete saved summaries from Saved Reports.

## Phase 2 · Provider hosted workflow

The country and investor classification entered by a user are self-declared. The handoff RPC independently requires a current provider connection and unexpired provider verification. A dated compliance version and agreement for the same provider and jurisdiction must be enabled. The application UI collects a document review acknowledgement and amount. The provider applies its own prescribed disclosures, identity checks and eligibility rules, takes the application and handles funds and withdrawal. A tracked handoff starts with a pending provider status.

Investor Profile & Applications shows provider verification separately from self-declared details. It shows the provider, jurisdiction, classification, verification and expiry dates, revoked connections and any recorded accountant-certificate review date. The profile response omits provider account identifiers and internal evidence references. It includes no certificate documents.

The service-role-only provider event RPC matches the connected provider account bound at handoff to the commitment. It rejects mismatched references and unsupported transitions, serializes updates, ignores replays and creates ownership atomically only on confirmation with issued units and settled capital. A second service-role RPC accepts dated settlement evidence, valuations, capital calls and distributions from a trusted adapter after its holding-activity approval is enabled. It checks account and currency, ignores replays and older revisions, and audits accepted changes. The app displays confirmed records as read-only. Manual records are labelled self-reported and remain device-local.

An adapter reverses a distribution with `record_private_holding_activity` and kind `distribution_reversal`. Send the original provider payment reference, holding, currency, provider event timestamp and reversal source. Pass null for amount and record date. The ledger retains the most recent payment evidence and reversal source, and writes an audit entry for each accepted reversal. A newer provider payment with the same reference can reinstate the distribution. The holdings API supplies only active distributions to the portfolio calculations.

For settled sales, `record_private_holding_disposition` accepts provider evidence for a holding bound to that provider account. It requires an unexpired agreement with both holding activity and disposition activity enabled. A signed, authenticated adapter supplies the stable sale reference, revision timestamp, units sold, net proceeds, settlement date and source, with cost basis when the provider supplies it. Corrections and reversals recompute remaining units atomically. Historical marks and cash flows remain visible. A partial sale requires a mark dated after the sale and reported after the latest revision. A reversal also requires a refreshed mark. A full exit retains its proceeds and source and is removed from current allocation.

Application details show the provider reference, original receipt, latest status, any withdrawal request and outcome, and the deadline supplied by the provider. Birchal and OnMarket applications link to their published withdrawal instructions. A provider-recorded withdrawal request keeps the application active and counts toward the retail cap until the provider confirms withdrawal or rejection. If the provider declines the withdrawal, the original application resumes. Settlement requires provider evidence and waits until the recorded withdrawal deadline has passed. Users can check withdrawal and refund status at the provider.

### AngelList capital account statements

Choose an authorized capital-account response and map one record’s identifier, fund name, currency, statement date and reported balances. The device requires the user to confirm the account belongs to them or their investing entity. A manager’s access to an LP record does not establish personal ownership. Values are copied from selected structured fields and stored in the signed-in Stock Tracker account’s data-protected local file. OAuth tokens remain in Keychain. No selected values are sent to Stock Tracker’s backend or to an AI model.

A dated NAV can contribute to unified allocation. Link an existing manual record for the same investment to avoid counting it twice. The newer valuation takes precedence, while the manual ledger remains available. Reimporting the same capital-account ID updates its snapshot and rejects an older statement or a currency change. The detail screen shows reported paid-in capital, commitment and distributions when supplied. Aggregate balances do not create dated cash flows or XIRR. Removing a saved statement or disconnecting AngelList removes its contribution to allocation.

### Provider onboarding

The Australian retail pilot starts with one provider-hosted CSF offer. Birchal's Financial Services Guide describes third-party referrals. OnMarket lists live offers and documents a commercial IPO integration with CMC Markets. Ask each provider's partnerships team whether its agreement can cover factual catalogue publication, provider-hosted applications and the return of signed application and holding records. OnMarket’s website terms reserve commercial use rights. ASIC’s CSF advertising guidance also applies to third-party publications. The public offers pages can identify pilot candidates for the partnership discussion. [Birchal contact](https://www.birchal.com/contact) · [OnMarket contact](https://www.onmarket.com.au/about-us/contact-us/) · [OnMarket offers](https://www.onmarket.com.au/offers/) · [OnMarket terms](https://www.onmarket.com.au/general/terms-of-use/) · [ASIC RG 262, paragraphs 113–115](https://download.asic.gov.au/media/kdljjy4d/rg262-published-18-october-2018-20260421.pdf)

The provider needs to supply the following for an acceptance run.

- The agreement scope, allowed jurisdictions and investor classes, published disclosure and warning links, rules for updating or removing offers, and the expiry of the authorization.
- A documented account linking and eligibility interface. Stock Tracker needs the stable provider subject, review outcome and expiry. The provider handles identity documents, AML/KYC and source-of-funds evidence, and returns the appropriate verification outcomes. For wholesale access, confirm how the provider represents the accountant-certificate review where it applies.
- Sandbox access and signed event documentation for application receipt, withdrawal windows and outcomes, rejection, confirmation, valuations and subsequent cash flows. Include event identifiers, correction and replay rules, signing key rotation and examples of currency, timezone and share precision.
- A monitored contact for discrepancies, failed delivery, revocations, privacy requests and incident escalation.

Use a sandbox investor to link the account, open the licensed provider's offer, review the disclosure, and submit there. Exercise the separate paths for confirmed ownership and withdrawn applications. Match each reference, unit count and amount back to the provider. Test duplicate delivery, a stale offer, a revoked investor, a different account and a corrected statement. Keep the exact sandbox evidence and sign-off with the agreement.

AngelList can verify the read-only path using an entitled account with a capital-account statement belonging to the test investor. Verify OAuth consent, the dated NAV and currency mapping, reimport and revocation against that account. Ask about Digital Subscriptions separately for an approved fund or syndicate subscription route. Syndex describes New Zealand registry and secondary market capabilities. Ask its partnerships team for the permitted investor jurisdictions and a registry-only interface before considering any Australian secondary pathway. ## Phase 3 · Advanced infrastructure

The product records capital calls, distributions, stale marks, share classes and dated corporate actions. Optional reminders schedule on the device for up to 20 upcoming recorded calls, at 9 a.m. the preceding day or the due date if the earlier reminder window has passed. The app clears pending reminders for settled calls after the provider records refresh. Capital payments, embedded execution, fund subscription, syndicate participation, live registry synchronization, and secondary transactions require their own provider agreements, documented APIs and jurisdiction review. There are no in-app payment or order routes. Stock Tracker does not act as a custody or secondary-market operator.

## Development and rollout

1. Apply migrations to a disposable project with `supabase start` or run `scripts/verify-migrations.sh`. Enable local Edge Runtime for the disposable project and serve the functions. The hosted Stock Tracker project has the Private Markets migrations and Edge Function. For updates, dry-run the linked migrations and deploy the `private-markets` function by name.
2. Configure the existing `CREDENTIAL_KEKS` and `CREDENTIAL_ACTIVE_KEK` secrets. Configure at least one supported model key using the existing AI gateway configuration. The private function shares the per-user AI quota. Keep older encryption keys available while retained documents and reports reference them.
3. Install a build with the Stock Tracker Supabase URL and publishable key. Test authentication, the closed catalogue, a user-created holding, and a research request in staging. The app's test build can use a local Supabase URL.
4. Before enabling `PRIVATE_MARKETS_DOCUMENT_AI_ENABLED`, review provider retention, cross-border handling, data-room terms and permission notices. Set `PRIVATE_MARKETS_DOCUMENT_AI_PROVIDER` to the approved provider, `openai` or `gemini`. Document runs use that provider exclusively. Keep the feature flag false when approval is absent.
5. Have compliance review the AU rule version, retail investor protections, wholesale routes, accountant-certificate review, documents, referral mechanics and user-facing AI. Keep handoff and provider activity disabled until the provider approves Stock Tracker and documents the applicable account, eligibility, confirmation, withdrawal and ledger interfaces. Add its adapter, test signatures and replay handling, and complete a provider sandbox acceptance run.
6. Keep live authorisations and contractual evidence outside this repository. Populate the compliance and approval records through privileged operational procedures after sign-off. The in-app cap accounts for provider applications known to Stock Tracker. The intermediary remains authoritative for the full per-company, per-intermediary rolling cap and cooling-off deadline.
7. The monitoring migration schedules `private-market-monitor` when `pg_cron` is installed. The collector processes opt-in accounts and reads published catalogue and confirmed provider records. It can also catch up on demand when an account opens its inbox.
8. The digest migration schedules `private-market-digest` every 15 minutes when `pg_cron` and `pg_net` are installed. Store the deployed Private Markets function URL as `private_market_digest_url` in Vault. Generate a dedicated secret and store the same value as `private_market_digest_secret` in Vault and `PRIVATE_MARKET_DIGEST_SECRET` in the Edge environment. The worker claims up to four reports per invocation, allows three total attempts per account each day, verifies publication and consent again before saving, and deduplicates previously summarised announcements. Inspect `cron.job_run_details` and the corresponding HTTP status in `net._http_response` to verify scheduling.
9. For APNs delivery, enable Push Notifications for `Cubeplay.Stock-Tracker` on the Apple Developer account and regenerate the development and distribution provisioning profiles. Set `PRIVATE_MARKET_APP_ENTITLEMENTS` to `Stock Tracker/Stock Tracker Push.entitlements` for the signed build. Set `PRIVATE_MARKET_APS_ENVIRONMENT` to `development` for a development profile or `production` for TestFlight and App Store distribution. Inspect the archived app signature and embedded profile to confirm the environment. The build defaults to the existing entitlements until APNs signing is provisioned.
10. Configure the three Edge secrets `PRIVATE_MARKET_APNS_SANDBOX_TEAM_ID`, `PRIVATE_MARKET_APNS_SANDBOX_KEY_ID` and `PRIVATE_MARKET_APNS_SANDBOX_PRIVATE_KEY` for the sandbox signing key. Use the corresponding `PRIVATE_MARKET_APNS_PRODUCTION_*` names for the production key. The `private-market-push` cron job uses the same private scheduler endpoint and secret as daily summaries. Without an APNs key for the requested environment, the app keeps its local notification path. Test with a signed iPhone, opt in to monitoring and device notifications, publish a verified update, verify one notification and open its sourced inbox entry, then test opt-out.
11. The `private-market-issuer-news` job uses the existing private scheduler endpoint to check the fixed AdvanCell newsroom. Successful fetches wait six hours before another check. The worker sends the archive’s ETag, bounds response size, refuses redirects, and backs off on source denial or throttling. Inspect the single row in `private_issuer_news_refresh` for the most recent check and any error. To stop publication, unpublish the relevant `corporate_actions` record. Imports preserve manual publication decisions. Review source terms and implement a dedicated adapter before adding another site.

The signed-in iPhone lifecycle tests use the current Edge Function and disposable Supabase Auth behind the local gateway at `127.0.0.1:58533`. Load the matching fixtures from `supabase/tests/private_application_lifecycle.sql` and `supabase/tests/private_provider_dispositions.sql` into a disposable database, leaving the relevant application or holding committed for the run. Create the test user through Auth and sign in to obtain a session. Supply the access and refresh tokens separated by a colon in the runner environment using `TEST_RUNNER_PRIVATE_LIFECYCLE_TEST_SESSION` and `TEST_RUNNER_PRIVATE_DISPOSITION_TEST_SESSION`. The app restores and validates the session through Supabase. Session injection is available only in a Debug UI test build on loopback. Delete the disposable users and fixture records after the run.

For the distribution inbox test, use `TEST_RUNNER_PRIVATE_DISTRIBUTION_TEST_SESSION` with a disposable account owning a confirmed holding and a recent provider-recorded distribution. The test opts in from Company Updates and checks the payment date and provider statement label.

For the research history simulator check, use `TEST_RUNNER_PRIVATE_RESEARCH_TEST_SESSION` with a disposable account that owns a current `running` attempt and another created more than three minutes ago. Both attempts must be within the seven-day display window.
